# Automatic Character Rigging in Blender: State of the Art, October 2026

Research pass for HIHO MOCAP Studio (marker-based auto-rigger, fixed 63-bone FreeMoCap skeleton, vendored MIT voxel heat-diffuse skinning). Checked 2026-10-15. Updates the May 2026 findings (Pinocchio family, RigNet/brignet dead, GameRig GPL-2 only, bone-heat as floor, meshonline voxel solver vendored). Nothing from that pass is repeated here except where it changed. ActionForge (actionforge.app) was studied separately on 10-13 and is mentioned in one line only.

Anything marked UNVERIFIED could not be confirmed on a first-party page.

## Bottom line

Auto-Rig Pro (now 3.78, $50 Full, GPL code plus proprietary AI binaries) is still the commercial reference for Blender. Its Smart workflow is exactly the shape HIHO already has: front-facing character, a handful of mirrored markers, optional AI guess, Go, Match to Rig, Bind, then named fix-up toggles for twist, hips, heels and head. The real movement since May is on the skinning side, not the skeleton side. Two new paid GPL add-ons (Adaptive Auto Skinning, July 2026, GPU voxel solver; WeightMend, October 2026, voxel visibility plus heat diffusion that tolerates broken meshes) and a fresh 4.3.0 release of meshonline's Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning (2026-10-09) all attack the same student problem HIHO has: multi-piece, non-watertight meshes that break Blender's bone heat. On the research side, the VAST-AI line (UniRig, then SkinTokens/TokenRig in Feb 2026, then AniGen) is MIT and strong, but every neural rigger needs an NVIDIA GPU with 8 to 14 GB VRAM and outputs its own skeleton, not a fixed one, so none can be the HIHO engine for old offline Macs today. The practical path for HIHO stays geometric: keep the marker-driven fit to the fixed skeleton, keep the vendored MIT voxel solver, and copy Auto-Rig Pro's interface sequence and per-region weight fix-ups. The one neural piece worth watching is SkinTokens' skin-an-existing-skeleton mode, which is the first MIT model that skins a skeleton you hand it.

## Per-tool notes

### Commercial and closed tools

**Auto-Rig Pro 3.78** (checked 2026-10-15). Sold on Superhive (formerly Blender Market): Lite $25 (core only), Full $50 (adds Smart, Remap, FBX/GLTF export), 10 seats $162, 20 seats $227, with 12 months of updates and 25 percent to extend. Supports Blender 2.93 through 5.2; 3.78 fixes a 5.1 crash and 5.2 setattr/getattr issues. Smart step list from the docs: (1) center the character at the origin facing minus Y, apply transforms, separate eyeballs for facial; (2) select body meshes, not props or clothes, click Get Selected Objects, the camera frames the character front-on; (3) place markers, Mirror on by default, either by clicking Add Neck and similar buttons or by Guess Markers (AI), which needs the proprietary AI files and only works on symmetric characters; the docs do not enumerate every marker type, so the exact list (neck, chin, shoulders, hands, root, feet, plus a head tip marker added in 3.76 and optional hand tip markers) is partly UNVERIFIED; (4) fingers, set count, then Guess Fingers (AI, tunable AI Samples and Error Threshold) or Voxel Centroid (runs with Go, tunable thickness and precision), spread fingers and palm facing the floor; (5) optional Add Facial markers plus eyeball/teeth/tongue object names; (6) Go! places reference bones; (7) Match to Rig builds the control rig; (8) Bind with engines Heat Maps (default, watertight), Voxelized (non-watertight, layered clothes), Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning (3rd party), and since 3.78 Adaptive Auto Skinning (3rd party). Bind toggles worth copying: Split Parts, Scale Fix, Optimize High Res, Selected Vertices Only, Apply Shape Keys, and the Smart-only fixers Refine Head Weights, Smooth Twist Weights, Improve Hips Weights, Improve Heel Weights. Body rig: modular fingers, toes, spine, neck, tail, ears, extra limbs, wings, kilts, spline IK, 3-bone IK; fingers grasp, fist, rotation-from-scale, IK/FK fingers, Smart IK pole (3.77). Facial rig with automated placement; 3.78 auto-detects the eyeball shape and avoids hair and eyelashes. Remap retargets BVH/FBX (Mixamo) onto any rig. Licensing (lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/license.html): source under GPL v3; the Auto-Rig Pro AI binaries are proprietary, non-transferable, one device at a time, no reverse engineering or training competing tools; icons and preset rig .blend parts are royalty-free but cannot be extracted; the generated rig .blend is the user's sole property; the FAQ says rigged characters can be sold if rig_tools and proxy_picker are bundled. Known weaknesses (docs FAQ, Blender Artists, ToolTrim 3.4/5): humanoids only for Smart; needs T or A pose and 1.0 scene scale; Smart crashes or freezes on some Macs (workaround: Disable Smart FX); finger detection fails on unusual hands; bind freezes on very high poly or many-piece meshes with Split Parts; non-manifold geometry, duplicate verts and imported custom normals produce bad weights; the FAQ itself says automatic binding "can never be perfect"; unapplied scale and arms crossing the body are the common user errors; support beyond 12 months costs extra. Sources: https://superhivemarket.com/products/auto-rig-pro , http://lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/auto_rig.html , https://lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/updates_log.html , https://www.lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/faq.html , https://lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/license.html , https://blenderartists.org/t/is-auto-rig-pro-unusable/1625185 , https://tooltrim.com/en/tool/auto-rig-pro/reviews

**Easy Rigify** (checked 2026-10-15). New in mid-2026, GPL-3.0-or-later per listing, Blender 4.2 to 5.2, Lite $15 (auto-detect without AI), Full $30 (adds "optional on-device AI-assisted detection" for body, fingers, face, plus Smart Bind, Surgical Weight Fix, Weight Cleanup, Joint Cleaner, Joint Corrective, cloth weight transfer, Unreal/Unity export). Four-step UI: Mark, Detect, Align, Generate, then Skin. Bipeds only. The detection method and marker count are not documented on the public page. Because it is GPL-3-or-later and its output is a plain Rigify metarig, it is the closest legal cousin to what HIHO is building; buying one seat to read the code is allowed under GPL. Source: https://superhivemarket.com/products/easy-rigify and /docs

**Adaptive Auto Skinning 1.4.11** (checked 2026-10-15). By Jessy Leite, $35, "GPL" on Superhive with no version shown (UNVERIFIED whether 3-or-later). Blender 4.2 to 5.2 on macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows, Linux; GPU via Metal, Vulkan, DirectX, no CUDA needed, Force CPU mode since 1.0.2, no external dependencies. Method: fill the character volume with voxels (92 base grid, subdivides where surfaces come close, e.g. between fingers), assign each voxel to a bone by skeleton shape not bone names, transfer to the surface; solves body and clothing together in one pass; handles non-watertight, doubled, overlapping meshes; writes standard vertex groups; live colour map of each bone's territory. Vendor benchmark: 0.57 s per full bind on a MacBook Air M4 and 80.9 percent dominant-bone agreement with hand-painted Mixamo references (vendor claim). Limit: face skinning only with ARP rigs. Auto-Rig Pro 3.78 added it as a bind engine. Sources: https://superhivemarket.com/products/adaptive-auto-skinning , https://80.lv/articles/try-this-one-click-automatic-skinning-add-on-for-blender

**WeightMend** (checked 2026-10-15). Published about a week ago, $15, "GPL" (version UNVERIFIED), Blender 4.2 to 5.0 tested. Bone visibility is tested against a voxel version of all selected meshes together, then heat diffusion is solved in a way that tolerates broken non-manifold geometry; handles holes, loose pieces, inner shells, overlapping parts, one or many meshes at once. Vendor test on 10 characters where Blender left parts unweighted. Source: https://superhivemarket.com/products/weightmend

**Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning 4.3.0 (commercial, meshonline)** (checked 2026-10-15). $30 indie, Blender 3.6 to 5.2, Linux/Windows/macOS including arm64, 4.3.0 dated 2026-10-09 adds Diffuse Amplify and Diffuse Decay and faster adaptive voxel diffusion. Dual-licensed: Python GPL, bundled C++ CLI explicitly NOT GPL. Recommends selecting all sub-meshes plus armature, and a two-pass recipe (Blender automatic weights for detailed areas, protected, then voxel for the rest). Bundles the free Surface Heat Diffuse add-on, the MIT one HIHO vendored; that open repo has had no push since 2024-08-31 (GitHub API). Sources: https://superhivemarket.com/products/voxel-heat-diffuse-skinning , https://api.github.com/repos/meshonline/Surface-Heat-Diffuse-Skinning

**ExitRig, SKAVA Auto Rigger Pro, Instant Rig & Animate** (checked 2026-10-15). Small paid "one-click" Blender add-ons ($9 to $20). SKAVA: select, Generate Rig, three rig types, automatic weight painting, "AI" is marketing copy. Instant Rig: reads the body mesh, estimates joints, IK hands and feet, "Smart Auto weights", bundles 15 clips. Listings say GPL for ExitRig and SKAVA; none verified. Sources: https://superhivemarket.com/products/exitrig/docs , https://superhivemarket.com/products/skava-auto-rigger-pro/faq , https://superhivemarket.com/products/instant-rig--animate-blender-auto-rig-addon-with-finger-rig-and-15-character-animations

**Mixamo** (checked 2026-10-15). Still online, free with an Adobe ID, no shutdown announced, but in maintenance mode with intermittent auth outages since June 2025 (vendor blogs, UNVERIFIED beyond user reports). FAQ requirements: humanoid with distinguishable head, body, arms, legs; no large extra appendages, wings, tails, big hair or clothing; neutral symmetric pose; nothing else in the file; only the last character is kept server-side. Marker set is chin, wrists, elbows, knees, groin, with symmetry, then a skeleton LOD choice (finger counts). Not available for enterprise/federated IDs or China. Adobe's own Blender add-on broke at 4.0; the community "Mixamo Rig" extension (GPL-3) replaces it. **Reallusion AccuRIG 2** (checked 2026-10-15). Free, Windows only (staff: no Mac plans; CrossOver lists it as will-not-install). Steps panel: load, check direction and centre line, Create Guides (auto-placed, then drag, depth from side view), set finger count and thumb angle, Generate Skeleton, mirror tools, Bind Skin (body and head only, no face), Check Animation with ActorCore motions (4,500 paid, 32 free). Exports FBX, USD, iAvatar. Staff say rigs of your own characters may be used commercially; EULA text not retrieved (UNVERIFIED). Sources: https://manual.reallusion.com/AccuRig-2/2.0/03-introducing-the-user-interface/steps-panel.htm , https://80.lv/articles/reallusion-launches-free-rigging-tool-accurig-2-0 , https://forum.reallusion.com/PrintTopic541835.aspx , https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/question-about-accurig-2s-eula/15034

**Cascadeur** (checked 2026-10-15). Free tier is non-commercial, exports only .casc (no FBX), 300 frames and 120 joints cap; Indie $8/mo annual, Pro $33/mo annual. Quick Rigging Tool: drag standard skeleton joints onto the mesh, Add rig elements. Not a Blender path. Source: https://cascadeur.com/plans , https://cascadeur.com/help/category/116

**Meshy, Tripo, Anything World, Kaedim** (checked 2026-10-15). Kaedim: quote-only, human in the loop. All cloud, none offline, none Blender-native. Sources: https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/guides/3d-model/rigging , https://studio.tripo3d.ai/workspace/rigging/ , https://www.awn.com/news/anything-world-launches-animate-anything-ai-rigging-and-animation-tool

### Open-source and research

**UniRig** (VAST-AI / Tsinghua, SIGGRAPH 2025; checked 2026-10-15). GitHub MIT (sidebar and API), HF weights MIT, 1.8k stars, 28 open issues, last push 2026-06-04. Two stages: autoregressive skeleton-tree transformer, then skin-weight prediction. Hardware: CUDA GPU with at least 8 GB VRAM, Python 3.11, spconv and torch_scatter (CUDA-only packages), flash_attn install issues; no CPU or Mac path in the README (UNVERIFIED negative). Checkpoint sizes: third-party conversions list ~1.4 GB skeleton and ~4.1 GB skin (UNVERIFIED). Output is its own skeleton per mesh, seed-dependent; no fixed-template mode. Blender: only a VRM import/export add-on forked from VRM-Addon-for-Blender. README now points to SkinTokens as successor. ComfyUI-UniRig wrapper exists (experimental). Sources: https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/UniRig , https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/UniRig , https://github.com/PozzettiAndrea/ComfyUI-UniRig

**SkinTokens / TokenRig** (VAST-AI, arXiv 2602.04805, Feb 2026; checked 2026-10-15). MIT code and MIT HF weights (two checkpoints, ~1.6 GB total). Created 2026-02-08, last push 2026-05-12, 493 stars, 10 issues. An FSQ-CVAE compresses sparse skin weights into discrete tokens so one autoregressive model emits skeleton and skinning together; claims 98 to 133 percent better skinning than prior work. Needs NVIDIA GPU 14 GB+, CUDA 12.1, Python 3.11. Has `--use_skeleton` to skin a mesh against an existing skeleton, which is the only MIT neural path that fits HIHO's fixed-skeleton design. Ships bpy_server.py and a Blender import note (remove glTF_not_exported node). Sources: https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/SkinTokens , https://huggingface.co/VAST-AI/SkinTokens , https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04805

**Make-It-Animatable v2** (CVPR 2025 Highlight; checked 2026-10-15). GitHub license MIT (API), HF weights apache-2.0 (earlier Space tag was CC-BY-NC, since changed). Last push 2026-09-08, 466 stars. Humanoid only, trained on Mixamo rigs so it produces Mixamo-style bones (exact bone count and finger coverage UNVERIFIED), under one second per character on GPU; v2 moved to a Hunyuan3D 2.1 ShapeVAE backbone, mesh input only, "Blender integration" in the demo flow. Inference VRAM not stated (training on A100 80 GB). The ComfyUI-UniRig wrapper recommends MIA for humanoids. Mixamo training data has its own terms (UNVERIFIED). Sources: https://github.com/jasongzy/Make-It-Animatable , https://huggingface.co/jasongzy/Make-It-Animatable

**MagicArticulate** (Seed3D/ByteDance, CVPR 2025; checked 2026-10-15). Apache-2.0 code and HF weights; dataset Articulation-XL2.0 CC-BY-4.0. Skeleton generation weights released April 2025; skinning stage partial. Last push 2025-09-14. GPU. Own skeleton. Source: https://github.com/Seed3D/MagicArticulate

**Puppeteer** (Seed3D/NTU, NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight; checked 2026-10-15). Apache-2.0, 429 stars, last push 2025-09-19, autoregressive skeleton plus attention skinning, community FBX export. Checkpoint release UNVERIFIED. Source: https://github.com/Seed3D/Puppeteer

**Anymate** (SIGGRAPH 2025; checked 2026-10-15). Repo github.com/yfde/Anymate has NO LICENSE file (API license null; top-level listing confirms). Third-party pages call it Apache-2.0 but that is not in the repo, so treat as all-rights-reserved until the authors add a file. 230K-asset dataset, three-stage joint/connectivity/skin pipeline, 8-GPU training config, needs Blender 4.0 for data. Last push 2025-08-28. Source: https://github.com/yfde/Anymate , https://api.github.com/repos/yfde/Anymate/contents/

**RigAnything** (Adobe, SIGGRAPH TOG 2025; checked 2026-10-15). LICENSE.md is the Adobe Research License: noncommercial research and teaching only, derivative works likewise. Not adoptable. Template-free, CUDA, GLB output via bpy. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Isabella98Liu/RigAnything/main/LICENSE.md

**AniGen** (VAST-AI, SIGGRAPH 2026; checked 2026-10-15). Generates animatable assets with unified shape/skeleton/skin fields; checkpoints on HF; GitHub license NOASSERTION (custom or none), created 2026-02-27, push 2026-07-15. Not adoptable until clarified. Source: https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/AniGen

**zeljkovranjes/auto-rigger** (checked 2026-10-15). A June 2026 pure-C# port for the s&box engine wrapping seven models (RigNet, UniRig, SkinTokens, MagicArticulate, Puppeteer, RigAnything, Anymate) and claiming the transformer stages run greedy-decode on CPU in one to several minutes per mesh. No license file, 2 stars. Not adoptable, but it is the only evidence found that these models can run without CUDA (UNVERIFIED). Source: https://github.com/zeljkovranjes/auto-rigger

### Free Blender add-ons and Blender itself

**Rigify in Blender 5.x** (checked 2026-10-15). No auto-fit and no new generator features in 5.0, 5.1 or 5.2. 5.0 added the Geometry Attribute constraint, per-instance bone visibility and selection, and a Python-breaking armature API; 5.1 shipped Rigify bugfixes (Generate Rig context failures, Human metarig asymmetry) and bone-collection fixes for extrude and Fill Between Joints; 5.2 LTS (July 2026) added Copy Constraints in Ctrl+L, Duplicate and Rename for bones, and Auto IK on non-connected parents. Metarig alignment is still manual per the manual. An extensions.blender.org mirror listing of Rigify shows "Disabled by staff" (UNVERIFIED why). Sources: https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.0/animation_rigging/ , https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.1/bugfixes/ , https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.2/animation_rigging/

**extensions.blender.org, Rigging tag** (72 add-ons; checked 2026-10-15). None claims automatic bone placement or automatic skinning. Relevant free ones: CloudRig (Blender Studio generator, GPL-3), Retarget 5.1.1 (fork of Expy Kit plus AnimAide, Blender 5+, GPL-3-or-later), EasyWeight (weight-paint QoL, GPL-3-or-later), Mixamo Rig (control rig over Mixamo FBX, GPL-3), MPFB (human generator with built-in rigs), Mesh2Rig (bakes deformations to bones, uses automatic weights), Rigi-All (Rigify helpers). ## Interface lessons for a novice-first auto-rigger

Every tool that works for beginners sequences the same way, and HIHO's Studio already follows it:

1. **Normalize first, silently.** ARP: centre at origin, face minus Y, apply transforms, Turn buttons. AccuRIG: check direction and centre line. ExitRig: Auto-Position. Mixamo: one character, nothing else in the file. Lesson: Studio should auto-centre, auto-face and apply scale before showing markers, and refuse (with a plain message) if scene scale is not 1.0 or if multiple armatures exist.
2. **Few markers, mirrored by default.** Mixamo uses five (chin, wrists, elbows, knees, groin) with symmetry; ARP roughly seven plus optional head and hand tips; HIHO uses 13. Mirror on by default, place only the left side. Lesson: consider collapsing HIHO's 13 to a one-side set with mirror, and show a count of remaining markers.
3. **Auto-guess, then let the user nudge.** ARP Guess Markers, AccuRIG Create Guides (auto-placed), Easy Rigify Auto Detect Body, Meshy auto-detect. The guess is always editable before anything is built. Lesson: a purely geometric guess (bounding box, extremity search, mirror plane) is enough to place first-pass markers; AI is optional polish.
4. **Fingers are a separate, optional sub-step** with a finger-count field and a one-sentence input rule (fingers spread, palm down). ARP ships two engines and tunables because finger detection is the step most likely to fail.
5. **Preview before bind.** AccuRIG Generate Skeleton then Check Animation; Meshy preview weights and T/A-pose test; ARP Go then Match to Rig then Bind as three distinct buttons. Lesson: show the fitted skeleton over the mesh and a quick pose test before committing weights.
6. **One Bind button with named fix-ups, not sliders.** ARP's Split Parts, Scale Fix, Smooth Twist Weights, Improve Hips, Improve Heel, Refine Head are checkboxes. Easy Rigify offers Surgical Weight Fix and Joint Cleaner. Lesson: expose three or four checkboxes with plain names, default on.
7. **Steps that fail most on messy student meshes** (from the ARP FAQ, Blender bug threads, Meshy troubleshooting): unapplied scale and rotation; posed or asymmetric characters; many separate pieces (bone heat fails with "failed to find solution for one or more bones", and ARP freezes with Split Parts on); non-manifold geometry, duplicate verts, imported custom normals; hidden inner geometry that heat cannot "see"; too-dense meshes; triangles after decimation; props and clothing included in the body selection; arms crossing the body; fingers not spread. The paid 2026 add-ons all pitch themselves on the "many pieces, not watertight" case, which confirms it is the number one failure for beginners.
8. **Face is always a separate pass** and often skipped (AccuRIG, Adaptive Auto Skinning, Meshy all defer face to manual).
